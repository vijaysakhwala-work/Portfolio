09 / SITE-WIDE DESIGN AND QUALITY GUARDRAILS
Responsive, fast, cohesive on every important page
•  Review the entire site—not only the homepage—for awkward blank areas, broken alignment, cramped type, poor card proportions, inconsistent button heights, weak information hierarchy and unnecessary scrollbars.
•  Test at approximately 320, 375, 390, 768, 1024, 1280, 1440 and 1920 px, including portrait/landscape behavior. Test Chrome, Safari/WebKit and Firefox where feasible.
•  Verify mobile nav, desktop dropdowns, touch targets, theme switcher, search, all carousels, form validation and submission, sticky elements, modals, anchor scrolling, lightbox and route transitions.
•  Use consistent typography (Inter/Poppins or the established design tokens), generous spacing, accessible contrast and an intentional hierarchy. Avoid any italic display copy and tiny text baked inside image assets.
•  Images should be crisp, unique where needed, compressed (WebP/AVIF as compatible), correctly cropped at common breakpoints and supplied at sensible resolutions. Prioritize meaningful alt text and no layout jumps.
•  Respect motion-reduction settings. Stop autoplay while the page is hidden/interaction is active when supported. Avoid large animation packages or unnecessarily expensive 3D effects.
•  Run the project build and relevant tests/lint checks. Check for console errors, missing assets, broken internal links, hydration issues, invalid markup and accidental regression of existing working sections.
