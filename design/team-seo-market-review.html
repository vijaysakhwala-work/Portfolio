Observed page content, not independently verified business claims; no measured ranking, traffic or authority comparison.

Toptal · Freelance talent marketplace; a different delivery model

Observed: Displays named developer profiles and specialisms, a matching process, a trial proposition, skill-specific destinations and hiring FAQs.

Content benchmark rather than a like-for-like staffing model or an independently validated talent claim.

iQlance · AI developer and project-team hiring

Observed: Publishes buyer answers about confidentiality, deliverable ownership, US collaboration, engagement pricing and getting started.

Implication for Cubusify: Answer those buyer questions using Cubusify’s actual agreements and operating practices. Do not copy another company’s availability, US-presence or onboarding promises.

Observed FAQ coverage; competitor promises and testimonials were not independently audited.

Prioritised content and measurement actions

  1. P1 · Connect the offer to genuine delivery evidence

    Add two relevant existing case-study links and explain the actual team contribution once confirmed. Keep proposed enterprise architectures labelled as proposals; do not treat them as production-stack proof.

    Review evidence: Main-content project links resolve and the accompanying responsibility statements are owner-confirmed.
  2. P1 · Make engagement selection useful

    Compare dedicated, project and flexible support by suitable situation, ownership of priorities, review cadence, scope boundaries and commercial arrangement. Publish only confirmed commitments.

    Review evidence: A buyer can explain which model fits their situation without relying on the labels alone.
  3. P1 · Add verified expertise and role context

    Introduce an actual delivery lead or approved practitioner information. Connect role cards to relevant existing service scope, and align button labels with whether they open detail or start a discussion.

    Review evidence: Role evidence is attributable; contextual service links work; button behaviour matches its wording.
  4. P2 · Answer the important hiring questions

    Cover how specialists are assessed, working-hour overlap, who manages the backlog, how scope/capacity is agreed, confidentiality and IP, continuity, starting arrangements and handover. Describe the actual process rather than borrowing competitor guarantees.

    Review evidence: Each FAQ resolves a distinct buying question with a substantive, accurate answer.
  5. P2 · Measure a real enquiry outcome

    Record an email-draft click separately from an enquiry received. Plan organic landing-session and qualified-enquiry reporting; use a confirmed receipt or CRM outcome for the business conversion.

    Review evidence: Reporting cannot count an opened mail application as a sent or qualified lead.
  6. P3 · Validate keywords and establish the acquisition baseline

    Obtain authorised US demand, backlink and first-party performance data before prioritising new hiring pages or setting numeric traffic goals.

    Review evidence: The baseline records the source, date range, market, exact page, query segmentation and data limitations.

Filter to the actual public team-page canonical and United States, Web search. Analytics and enquiry outcomes
Filter landing-page sessions to the team route and Organic Search; distinguish session geography from Search Console country filters. Review engaged sessions and downstream enquiry outcomes with consistent attribution and consent coverage.
Backlink evidence
Inspect the exact canonical and URL variants alongside domain-wide links. Review unique referring domains, topical relevance, actual referring pages, anchor patterns, follow/nofollow attributes and new/lost links. Compare equivalent competitor landing pages without treating a vendor authority score as a Google metric.
US query demand
Retrieve US query volumes and difficulty with provider/date/method stated. Check commercial versus job-search intent and seasonal movement before mapping one primary query to the page.
Version attribution
