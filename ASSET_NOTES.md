# Project imagery

The healthcare, ecommerce, manufacturing, logistics, education, finance, real-estate and media images are AI-generated illustrative photography. They must not be described as photos of Cubusify employees or clients.

The product image is AI-generated and represents sample catalogue items only.

The logo source is the Cubusify branded asset retrieved from the user's earlier work. Only the brand mark is repeated as branding; decorative cubes are not used across the website.

Inter font files and their included OFL license are retained locally so the deployed site does not depend on Google Fonts requests.


## Selected redesign reference

The local `industry-*.webp` images and `shoe-concept.png` are extracted from the latest approved Cubusify homepage design, “Cubusify: Software your business runs on.png.” They are illustrative generated reference photographs, not pictures of Cubusify clients or employees. The original project photographs remain available in `public/images/`. These extracted reference images are intentionally small; use higher-resolution approved assets when larger photo displays are needed.
